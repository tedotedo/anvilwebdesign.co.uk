Questions

Straight answers.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I build a working version of your home page so you can see what I'd do. If it's not for you, that's the end of it, and I won't keep chasing you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain and hosting are set up in your name from the start, and the site is yours once it's paid for. If you ever want to move on, you take everything with you.

What does it cost to run?

For a normal site, hosting costs nothing and the domain is about £10 a year. The care plan is optional.

How long does it take?

Usually within two weeks of getting your words and photos. Bigger sites take longer, and I'll give you a date before we start.

What do you need from me?

What you do, who for, your prices and some photos. I'll send you simple questions to answer rather than asking you to write a website, and I'll tidy up the wording.

Will I show up on Google?

Every site is set up so Google can read it properly, and I'll tidy your Google Business Profile to match. Nobody can honestly promise a position, so I won't, but I'll fix what's holding you back.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or better rebuilt, and I can move it into your own name if someone else holds it.
