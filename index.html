How it works

  1. You tell me about your business, by email, through the form below, or over a coffee.
  2. I build a mockup of your home page and send you a link. That part is free.
  3. If you'd like to go ahead, I give you a fixed price in writing. You pay half to start and half when the site is ready.
  4. You send me your words and photos, and I finish the site. It's usually live within two weeks.

The website and the web address are registered in your name, not mine, so they're yours to keep whatever happens.

Questions people ask

Is the mockup really free?
Yes. If it isn't for you, that's fine, and I won't keep chasing you.
Will I own the website?
Yes. Once it's paid for, it's yours, and the web address is in your name from the start. If you ever want someone else to look after it, you can take everything with you.
Will it work on phones?
That's where I start. Most of your customers will look you up on a phone first.
Will I show up on Google?
I set every site up properly for Google and help you tidy your Google Business Profile. Nobody can honestly promise a position, so I won't. If you'd like help after launch, I can keep your Google profile up to date, add new photos and posts, and check how you're showing up in searches, as part of the monthly plan.
I've already got a website. Can you help?
Probably. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or better rebuilt.
