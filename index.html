05 — Questions

Plain answers.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I build a working version of your home page so you can see what I'd do. If it isn't for you, that's the end of it, and I won't keep chasing you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain and hosting are in your name from the start, and the site is yours once it's paid for. If you ever move on, everything goes with you.

What does it cost to run?

For a normal site, hosting costs nothing and the domain is about £10 a year. The care plan is entirely optional.

How long does it take?

Usually within two weeks of receiving your words and photos. Larger sites take longer, and I'll give you a date before we begin.

What do you need from me?

What you do, who for, your prices and some photographs. I'll send simple questions rather than asking you to write a website, and I'll shape the wording with you.

Will I show up on Google?

Every site is set up so Google can read it properly, and I'll tidy your Google profile to match. Nobody can honestly promise a position, so I won't, but I'll fix what's holding you back.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth improving or better rebuilt, and help move it into your own name if someone else holds it.
